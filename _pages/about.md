I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am advised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
